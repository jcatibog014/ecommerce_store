How the File Looks

One row for each unit. Rows with the same name are one product. Prices are in pesos.

name,brand,kind,category,unit,pieces,retail,wholesale,stock
Soy sauce 1 L,,,Condiments,Bottle,1,48,45,48
Soy sauce 1 L,,,Condiments,Case of 12,12,560,530,
3-in-1 coffee sachet,Kape Juan,Creamy White,Drinks,Sachet,1,12,10.50,300

Brand and kind are optional. The same name with another brand or kind is another kind of the product, with its own prices and stock.

New products get the stock from their first row. Products you already have keep their stock; only their prices change.
